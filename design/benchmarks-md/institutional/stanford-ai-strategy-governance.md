#### **Navigate the strategic** **decisions behind successful** **AI transformation**

For many organizations, the question is no longer whether to adopt AI, but how to create
lasting strategic value from it. Senior leaders are responsible for setting direction, making
investment choices, and ensuring that AI initiatives support broader business priorities.
Success depends on more than understanding the technology; it requires the judgment to
turn AI potential into meaningful business outcomes.


AI is changing how organizations operate and make decisions. Leaders must assess where
it can create the greatest value, manage the trade-offs involved, and build alignment
across the enterprise. They must also create conditions for innovation without losing sight
of accountability and risk.


As generative AI and agentic AI become more embedded in business, executives need a
broader strategic perspective. They must be able to identify high-value opportunities,
prepare the organization for AI at scale, and put effective governance in place. The ability
to connect strategy with execution is becoming a defining leadership capability in AIdriven transformation.

### **About this** **program**

The **AI for Senior Executives** program from
Stanford Online is designed for those
responsible for shaping strategy, guiding
organizational transformation, and creating
long-term enterprise value.

The program explores how AI technologies,

By the end of the program, participants will be
equipped to anticipate AI trends, identify
emerging opportunities, make informed

responsible organization-wide transformation.

### **A blended format** **designed for** **executive learning**

This program combines flexible asynchronous learning, online engagement, and a 3.5-day
on-campus immersion at Stanford University. The format is designed to give leaders
access to Stanford faculty, strategic AI frameworks, Silicon Valley perspectives, and
executive peer exchange while supporting learning alongside professional responsibilities.
Sessions, both online and on campus, create opportunities for real-time discussion and
relationship building, while asynchronous content allows leaders to engage with core
concepts at their own pace.


**Learn from Stanford faculty**


Gain insights from Stanford faculty at the intersection of AI, strategy, leadership,
and organizational transformation. Experience Silicon Valley’s innovation
ecosystem through exposure to emerging research and young AI startups and
explore how new technologies can translate into business opportunities while
supporting ethical, safe, and responsible AI adoption at scale.


**Responsible AI leadership**


Examine AI safety, ethics, trust, governance, and enterprise controls through a
senior leadership lens, balancing innovation with accountability and
responsible adoption at scale.


**Blended learning journey**


Engage with a learning format that combines self-paced study, online sessions,
and in-person immersion at Stanford University. This structure supports deeper
engagement, peer interaction, and applied discussion while giving senior leaders
the flexibility to continue managing their professional responsibilities.


**Earn three Stanford Online credentials**


Receive two digital badges for completing **AI-Driven Leadership: Strategies for**
**the Future** and **Futures Thinking for Strategic Decision-Making**, and a digital
Professional Certificate for completing **AI for Senior Executives** .

### **Your instructors** **at a glance**

**Saikat Chaudhuri**


**Professor of Management Science and**
**Engineering, Stanford University and**
**Academic Director, Stanford**
**Technology Ventures Program (STVP)**


Saikat Chaudhuri was the inaugural academic director of the Management, Entrepreneurship &
Technology (M.E.T.) Program and the Berkeley Haas Entrepreneurship Hub at the University of
California, Berkeley. Saikat’s research specializes in corporate growth and innovation strategies,
particularly high-tech mergers and acquisitions, high-value outsourcing and partnerships, and
technological innovation in dynamic environments. His publications span both scholarly and
managerial outlets, and his work has been recognized from the field’s leading professional
associations including the Academy of Management and Strategic Management Society. He has
been the recipient of numerous teaching awards and accolades at all levels across his academic
career. Saikat’s work has been cited by leading media, and he is often interviewed or quoted by
the global press on contemporary business affairs. He is regularly invited to speak at corporate
forums, industry events, and academic conferences across the United States, Europe, and Asia.
Based on his research and experience, he advises leading global corporations and governments
worldwide, including providing expert legal testimony. He earned his bachelor's degrees in
mechanical engineering and multinational management from the Jerome Fisher Program in
Management & Technology at the University of Pennsylvania, master’s degree in manufacturing
systems engineering from Stanford University, and a doctorate in technology and operations
management from Harvard University.

Mykel Kochenderfer is Associate Professor of Aeronautics and Astronautics at Stanford

airspace modeling and aircraft collision avoidance, with his early work leading to the
establishment of the ACAS X program. He received a Ph.D. from the University of Edinburgh and

applications where decisions must be made in uncertain, dynamic environments while
maintaining safety and efficiency. Research at SISL focuses on efficient computational methods
for deriving optimal decision strategies from high-dimensional, probabilistic problem
representations. He is an author of "Decision Making under Uncertainty: Theory and
Application" (2015), "Algorithms for Optimization" (2019), and "Algorithms for Decision Making"
(2022), all from MIT Press. He is a third generation pilot.
