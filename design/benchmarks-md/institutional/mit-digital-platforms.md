Decision

Marking


Despite their reputation for rapidly expanding

customer bases, the long-term success of

digital platform models depends more on

how they’re implemented. Namely, sustained

impact is often derived from the technical
expertise and effective decision-making

processes of managers and technical

business leaders looking to grow their

businesses in a customer-centric way.

entrepreneurs—a substantial competitive

advantage.

Key Takeaways


This course is designed to help participants build their own successful digital platforms.


It focuses on technical and design aspects, as well as on the importance of the effective

decision-making processes that participants will encounter when developing a digital

platform. These types of decisions may include when to open a platform, how to use APIs to

build an ecosystem of partners, how to shape an architecture in an open environment, and
how to find new customers in a digital platform.


The course is designed with practicality in mind and includes several case studies of
successes and failures in modern markets.


Throughout this course, participants will:



01 Recognize two-sided markets and

distinguish them from traditional

onecsided or direct buyer-seller markets.


02 Critically analyze and discuss examples

of industry platforms such as eBay,

Etsy, Apple, Amazon, and Alibaba, and

determine factors for success and

failure.


03 Identify the technical choices a firm
makes in creating APIs and industry

standards for two-sided markets

enabled by digital platforms.


7



04 Plan for development and rollout of a
two-sided market, including adding
features, incentivizing key customers,

and negotiating with partners.


05 Examine the most recent research
and models on two-sided markets,
including on the stability of two-sided

markets.


06 Define metrics for success in adoption

and customer engagement.


07 Frame the tactics for growing provider

and customer bases simultaneously
versus the firm’s balance sheet.

Structure of a

Typical Digital
Platform and

Two-sided Market



The demand side on the left side shows

potential users, who may become users

when they join the digital platform to

purchase goods or services over the

platform. The supply side on the right side

consists of potential providers who become

actual providers once they join the platform.


8



Transactions are mediated over the digital

platform which has an owner who monetizes

the volume of transactions and the current

user and provider base. The increase (or

decrease) in users and adopters is mediated

by a number of factors such as network
effects, as well as the value, quality and price
of the goods and services offered over the

platform

Entrepreneurs looking to develop startups centered around digital platforms and two-sided

Policy experts and regulator who work with digital market policies in an expanding global

Professionals from technical backgrounds who work with digital platforms and two-sided

markets who want to understand the choices they can make to enable their success.

Real-World

Applications


The high consumer demand for two-sided markets and

online marketplaces means that the demand for

engineers and developers who can build and operate

digital platforms will increase as well. These

professionals should be equipped with the latest

technical knowledge and understand which technical

choices can greatly increase the possibilities for

business success.


With the rise and proliferation of digital platforms and

two-sided markets, an increasing number of

entrepreneurs are looking to launch startups based on
digital platforms.


However, increasing competition, insufficient technical

knowledge, and confusion regarding the dynamics of

two-sided markets and online marketplaces could be
fatal for their business operations. This course offers

entrepreneurs the opportunity to acquire the expertise

they need to gain a strategic advantage as they plan for

and develop a new business.
