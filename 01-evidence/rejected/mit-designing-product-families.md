Course Outline


How do we conceptualize Platforms at MIT Professional Education?

For the Faculty of this program, a platform is the productive space of a family of products

in which all these products or services share a series of common components,

technologies and know-how applied in order to save production costs, optimize the
supply chain and increase company profits by sharing common elements.


Module 1
Fundamental Principles of Platform-Based Manufacturing

In this module, we will look at some of the conceptual foundations on which platform

systems are understood. We will have the opportunity to learn about the ways in which
different companies have established a conception of design and industrial production

based on platforms and exploitation methodologies. We will approach some case studies

of real companies and products. After this, we will analyze the paradoxes and
contradictions that arise in this field. Although platforms offer many opportunities, going
through the different phases prior to their creation process involves several difficulties.
