Key Takeaways

This course is designed to refine participants’ communication and interpersonal skills,
allowing clear, precise information to be delivered in an effective and persuasive manner. It

also aims to guide highly skilled technical professionals to adapt their message to a nontechnical audience.


The skills and knowledge presented in this course can be applied across multiple

industries, including engineering, tech, human resources, sales, sustainability, and
entrepreneurship, among others.


Due to its extensive application and the increasing need for universally effective
communication, the expertise on offer can also be of great value in participants’ own
personal lives.


Throughout this course you will:



01 Learn about disparate

communication styles and how
they influence attitudes and

behaviors.


02 Discover the psychology behind

persuasion and implement it in

your own professional

interactions.


03 Understand which characteristics

make a great public speaker and

learn to apply them to yourself.


04 Master how to adapt your message
to different audiences, including
those with differing levels of

technical knowledge.


7



05 Explore the impact of digitalization
in communication and learn to
manage virtual communications in
an effective, powerful manner.


06 Find out which processes are
behind critical thinking and how to

use them to upgrade your message.


07 Comprehend the importance of
data visualization to clarify your

data for any kind of audience.


08 Devise a communication strategy

for your organization.

psychology, and law journals.

research in three areas: classical rhetoric,

contemporary rhetorical theory and
