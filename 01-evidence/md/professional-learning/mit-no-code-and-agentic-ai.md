**Deep Learning and Neural Networks**


Learn the fundamentals of deep learning, including the building blocks of neural networks, their structure, and how


they learn from data. Explore key concepts such as layers, activation functions, and training processes, and apply


them to practical tasks like digit recognition. Build a foundational understanding of how neural networks are designed,


trained, and used in real-world AI applications.


**Computer Vision Methods**


Explore how AI systems interpret and analyze visual data, starting with the limitations of traditional neural networks in


image-based tasks. Understand the building blocks of convolutional neural networks and how they are designed to


capture spatial patterns and features in images. Examine how these models are trained and optimized, and apply them


to practical use cases such as image detection. Build a clear understanding of how modern computer vision systems


are designed and used in real-world scenarios.


**Ethical and Responsible AI**


Understand the principles of building AI systems that are ethical, transparent, and accountable. Explore the AI


lifecycle and examine how bias can arise at different stages, along with real-world examples. Cover key concepts such


as causality, privacy, and broader interconnections across domains that influence AI outcomes. Develop the ability to


critically evaluate and design AI solutions that are responsible and trustworthy by understanding interdependencies


and feedback loops within AI systems.


**Data Exploration: Temporal Data**


Understand time series as a unique data modality that requires specialized techniques for analysis. Explore the key


components of time series data, including trend, seasonality, and noise, and how to identify and estimate these


patterns. Cover foundational methods for time series forecasting, enabling analysis of temporal data and generation of


informed predictions for real-world applications.

##### **About MIT Professional Education**

For more than 75 years, MIT Professional Education has been providing

technical professionals worldwide a gateway to renowned MIT research,

knowledge, and expertise, through advanced education programs

designed specifically for them. In addition to industry-focused,

two-to-five day live online and on-campus courses through Short

Programs, MIT Professional Education offers professionals the

opportunity to take online and blended learning courses through Digital

companies. For more information, please visit _professional.mit.edu._
