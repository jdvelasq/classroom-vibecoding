###### About this Course

Optimize technology and take advantage of MIT Professional

Education’s knowledge, expertise, and hands-on approach.

Drive your professional trajectory at full speed into the future

of industry. Smart manufacturing is about increasing
efficiency and eliminating pain points in the system. It’s

characterized by a highly connected, knowledge-enabled

industrial enterprise where all organizations an operating

systems are linked, leading to enhanced productivity,

sustainability, and economic performance.


In keeping with MIT’s founding principle Mens et Manus (Mind and Hand)—the synergy of

theory and practice being at the heart of the learning experience— participants will learn by
doing.


The course centers around a smart machine—a fiber extrusion device, fondly referred to as

FrED—to demonstrate concepts as well as incorporate problemsolving skills into the

curriculum. Furthermore, participants will have a chance to engage with their peers and

expert learning facilitators to explore how these concepts can be leveraged in their

organization.


10 weeks 8-10 hours a week Online

###### Key Takeaways

01 Visualize the production of data to

better understand the key variables and

phenomena underlying manufacturing.


02 Control of the manufacturing

processes, feedback, process

modeling, and monitoring.


03 Application of advanced data analysis

to prepare statistic models and test

manufacturing processes.


04 Development of models to make sense

of data, and to examine and improve

FrED.


05 Discovery of the role played by sensors

in data production and smart

manufacturing, and evaluation of the

types of data that can be produced.


6



06 Evaluation of sensors and their

respective data.


07 Analysis of the differences between the

actual and predicted dynamics.


08 Deepen understanding in innovation of
machines and artificial vision. Learn
more about the different iterations

of FrED, whose objective is to provide

examples of optimizing machines.


09 Examination of the discovery process

of data that best adjust to a model. The
adaptation of models and sensitivity

analysis establishes the connection
between artificial vision and the control

of statistical processes.


10 Completion of an advanced data

analysis using datasets produced by

FrED during the manufacturing process.

###### Participant Profile

This course is somewhat technical in nature, however this course material is highly

accessible for those new to smart manufacturing concepts, while also being valuable for

those who already have some experience. There are no prerequisites for this course. It is

designed for:


Plants, production and operations managers who work in the manufacturing sector.


Data scientists who want to put their capabilities into practice in the
field of smart manufacturing.


Design and manufacturing engineers who are looking to learn about data and development

models in the manufacturing sector.


Consultants whose objective is to put additional value on the latest

innovative technology in the manufacturing sector.


It is highly recommended that functional and cross-functional teams

carry out the course together in order to accelerate the process of

adopting smart manufacturing practices.

###### Instructors

Dr. Brian W. Anthony


Director of the MIT Master of Engineering in

Advanced Manufacturing and Design. Associate

Director, MIT.nano


Dr. Anthony is the director of the MIT Master

of Engineering in Advanced Manufacturing &

Design, and codirector of the Medical
Electronic Device Realization Center. He has

more than 20 years of experience in product

realization and is an Emmy award winner in

the Academy of Television Arts & Sciences
for his innovation in broadcast technology.


Dr. Anthony designs instruments and
techniques to monitor and control physical

systems. He also uses mechanical,



electrical, and optical engineering as well as

computer science and optimization to
develop solutions.
Dr. Anthony’s research revolves around the
design of instruments and techniques for
measuring and controlling complex physical
systems. It includes the development of
instruments and measurement solutions

for manufacturing, medical diagnostics, and

imaging diagnostics. In addition to his

academic work, he has extensive

experience in market-driven technological

innovation, product development and

entrepreneurship, and marketing at the

point where information technology and

advanced manufacturing come into contact.

As an educator, his main interests are the
modeling of large-scale systems dealing

with decision-making and the development

of optimization algorithms and software to

analyze and design these systems. He is an

expert in market-driven technological
innovation as well as in entrepreneurship.


Dr. Anthony holds the following degrees:

- PhD in Engineering, Massachusetts
Institute of Technology

- MS in Engineering, Massachusetts Institute
of Technology

- BS in Engineering, Carnegie Mellon

University broadcast technical innovation.



“Using technology to refine the manufacturing process
has been a steady interest throughout my career and allows better
products to be offered on the market, faster and more

economically.”

###### Certificate of Completion



All participants who successfully complete

Smart Manufacturing: Moving From Static
to Dynamic Manufacturing Operations will

receive an MIT Professional Education
Certificate of Completion. In addition, they

will also earn 8 Continuing Education Units

(MIT CEUs).



In order to obtain CEUs, participants must
complete a required CEU confirmation form.

CEUs are calculated based on the number of

learning hours in each course.



The Continuing Education Unit (CEU) is defined as 10 contact hours of ongoing learning to indicate the

amount of time they have devoted to a non-credit/non-degree professional development program. To
understand whether or not these CEUs may be applied toward professional certification, licensing

requirements, or other required training or continuing education hours, please consult your training

department or licensing authority directly.
