### **THE MIT PROFESSIONAL EDUCATION ADVANTAGE**

The mission of MIT Professional Education is to provide a gateway to renowned MIT research,
knowledge, and expertise for those engaged in science and technology worldwide, through
advanced education programs designed for working professionals.


MIT Professional Education supports MIT’s vision, including connecting practitioner-oriented
education with industry. MIT Professional Education provides continuing education courses and
lifelong learning opportunities for science, engineering, and technology professionals at all levels,
from around the world. Worthy of note is that MIT professors and lecturers lead and teach all MIT
Professional Education offerings. MIT Professional Education fosters the development of
innovative leaders equipped to address complex problems globally.

### **CURRICULUM**

**KEY MODULES**


**AI BACKGROUND AND SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW**


You will be taken through a brief history of AI, while diving deep into the AI domains of
impact. You will also be introduced to the AI system architecture and address the key
building blocks that form an end-to-end AI pipeline. You will master the challenges and key
building blocks necessary for developing AI system capabilities.


**AI STRATEGIC VISION AND IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP**


You will be introduced to a strategic development model. Using this framework, you will
learn to address the formulation of a strategic vision, product and/or service value
proposition, and the important components of an AI development roadmap. Overall, you will
learn to formulate a strategic development plan that serves as the guiding blueprint for AI
designers, developers, and customers.


**UNDERSTANDING DATA, CONDITIONING, AND PREPARING DATA**


In building AI and ML capabilities, a significant amount of time is spent conditioning the
data to be ready for the application of ML algorithms. You will learn to architect systems to
transform structured and unstructured data into information. You will gain the tools and
know-how required to recognize the vulnerability of a system to data bias.


**INTRODUCTION TO MACHINE LEARNING AND STRATEGIC APPLICATIONS**


You will be introduced to machine learning and gain a deeper understanding of different
methods of machine learning. You will learn how the different classes of ML algorithms
transform information into knowledge. Here you will gain experience developing a simple

neural network as well.


**IMPLEMENTING MACHINE LEARNING IN A BUSINESS – PLANNING, MONITORING,**

**EVALUATING AND CONSTRAINTS**


Recognize and learn how you need to pivot everything from work processes to thought
processes and integrate machine learning into the planning, monitoring, evaluating and
implementation of business at every level. You will also learn to gauge the limitations and
challenges associated with machine learning in business and how you can address and

resolve those issues.

### **CURRICULUM**

**PREDICTIVE ANALYSIS AND DECISION SYSTEMS**


Build the dexterity needed to extract insights from data and subsequently to make decisions
that impact day-to-day mission-critical operations, as well as for long-term. Develop a deep
understanding and hands-on experience of different methods to build decision systems and
predictive capabilities.


**MODERN COMPUTING FRAMEWORKS**


Understand how recently developed computing technologies or domain specific architectures
(DSAs) such as the Graphics Processing Units (GPUs) and the Tensor Processing Units
(TPUs), enable narrow AI to be more effective. Understand what makes GPUs and TPUs
well-matched with executing machine learning algorithms. Develop the capability to assess
various computing technologies suited to your needs in terms of computation throughputs.


**HUMAN-MACHINE TEAMING**


Learn how human-machine teaming transforms the output of the ML algorithms from
knowledge into insight for users to leverage the output of the AI and ML systems. You will
develop the skills needed to classify areas where the application of AI augments the
capabilities of humans working together as teams with the machine (or chiefly computer).


**BUILDING AND DEPLOYING ROBUST AI CAPABILITIES**


Develop the ability to assess the robustness of the AI system and ascertain the
trustworthiness throughout its life cycle to be able to successfully deploy an AI system. You
will learn about assessment of the sensitivity of ML algorithms to modifications of data and
the algorithm model itself. You will also be introduced to a set of ten guidelines that involve
people, processes, and technologies, and are necessary in the design, development, and
implementation of end-to-end AI capabilities.


**COMMUNICATING VALUE AND MANAGING STAKEHOLDERS**


As C-suite executives, product managers, implementation and business leaders, it is
important to be able to communicate the value proposition of AI products or services. You
will master the skills and techniques, based on a proven methodology, to communicate the
value proposition of AI products or services clearly and concisely.
